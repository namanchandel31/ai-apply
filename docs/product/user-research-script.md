# OneTap MVP — User Research Script & Scorecard

Use the same script for every participant so results stay comparable across sessions.

---

## Session overview

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Duration** | 35–45 min |
| **Format** | Video call + screen share (or async Loom + 20 min debrief) |
| **Goal** | Learn where OneTap helps, where it breaks trust/flow, and what to build next |
| **Your role** | Listen, observe, don't demo unless they're completely stuck for 60+ seconds |

---

## Before the call (send 24h ahead)

**Message template:**

> Hey [Name] — thanks for trying OneTap 🙏  
> Before we chat, could you spend ~15 min doing **one real task**?  
> 1. Log in and complete Setup if you haven't (resume + email + AI)  
> 2. On **Apply**, paste a **real job description** you'd actually apply to  
> 3. Try to get it to "sent" or "ready to review" (your choice: auto-apply on or off)  
> 4. Open **Applications** and find that job  
> Optional: if you use LinkedIn for jobs, try the extension + **Add to OneTap** on a hiring post  
> During the call I'll ask you to **think out loud** — no wrong answers, brutal honesty helps most.

**Screening (quick, in WhatsApp or start of call):**

1. Are you **actively job hunting** right now? (yes / passive / not now)
2. Roughly how many applications per week?
3. How do you track apps today? (spreadsheet, Notion, nothing, etc.)
4. Have you used OneTap before this session? (first time / tried once / regular)

Label each person **Active hunter** vs **Feedback-only** in your notes — don't mix them in synthesis.

---

## Call structure

| Phase | Time | What you do |
|-------|------|-------------|
| Intro & consent | 3 min | Set expectations, ask to record |
| Pre-task recap | 2 min | What they tried before the call |
| **Live task** | 12–15 min | They share screen; you stay quiet |
| **Core questions** | 15–18 min | Same 7 questions every time |
| Wrap | 2 min | #1 priority + follow-up ask |

---

## Live task (pick ONE per participant)

**Task A — Paste JD (core flow)**  
*"Use a real JD. Paste it on Apply, get as far as you can toward sending or reviewing the email. Then find it on Applications."*

**Task B — No contact email**  
*"Use a JD that doesn't include a recruiter email. Show me what OneTap does and what you'd do next."*

**Task C — Extension (if they use LinkedIn)**  
*"Find a hiring post on LinkedIn and use Add to OneTap. Then check Applications."*

**Task D — Review mode**  
*"Turn auto-apply off. Paste a JD, edit the email if you want, and send or stop where you'd stop in real life."*

Note which task you used on the scorecard.

## Intro script (read verbatim)

> "I'm researching OneTap — not testing you. There are no wrong answers.  
> Please **think out loud**: 'I'm looking for…', 'I'm not okay with this because…'  
> I'll stay quiet during the task on purpose — if you're stuck for more than a minute, say so and we'll talk about that moment.  
> Is it okay if I record for my notes?"

---

## The 7 core questions (ask in order)

### Q1 — Baseline workflow

**"Before OneTap — walk me through the last time you applied to a job, from finding the posting to hitting send. What was the most annoying or time-consuming part?"**

*Listen for:* copy-paste, tracking, finding contact, writing cover email, follow-up.

---

### Q2 — Tracking & chaos

**"How are you keeping track of applications today? What breaks first when you're applying to more than a few jobs per week?"**

*Listen for:* whether Applications tab solves a real job vs. nice-to-have.

---

### Q3 — First impression (after they've seen the product)

**"In your own words — what is OneTap for, and what would you try first if a friend sent you the link?"**

*Listen for:* mental model (email tool vs. tracker vs. auto-applier vs. LinkedIn helper).

---

### Q4 — Friction moments (main research question)

**"Where did you get stuck, hesitate, or feel unsure you were doing the right thing? Can you point to the exact screen or step?"**

*Probe at these OneTap moments if they don't mention them:*

- Setup / onboarding (resume, email, AI)
- Apply tab — paste JD, preview, auto-apply toggle
- Missing contact email → "Needs review"
- Applications tab — finding the row, status column, filters
- Send email / Continue send
- Extension connect or LinkedIn button

*Follow-ups (use on every answer):*

- "What did you expect to happen there?"
- "How often does that happen in your job search?"
- "What do you do today when that happens?"

---

### Q5 — Helpful vs. risky

**"What felt genuinely helpful vs. what felt risky or too automatic — especially around AI-written emails, auto-send, or sending without a contact email?"**

*Listen for:* control, edit-before-send, trust in AI tone, fear of wrong recipient.

---

### Q6 — Expectation gap

**"Pick one moment — paste JD, email preview, Applications tab, or extension — what did you expect to happen next, and what actually happened?"**

*Force one specific moment — comparable across users.*

---

### Q7 — Forced prioritization

**"If we could only ship one improvement in the next two weeks to make this worth using every day while job hunting, what would it be — and why that one over everything else?"**

*Listen for:* one clear bet, not a wish list.

---

## Wrap questions (every call)

1. **"Would you use OneTap for your next 3 real applications? Why or why not?"**
2. **"Anything I didn't ask that I need to hear?"**
3. **"Can I follow up in a week after you've tried 2–3 more real apps?"** (yes / no)

## After 5–8 sessions — synthesis template

Tag each note with one primary theme:

| Theme | Example signal |
|-------|----------------|
| **Onboarding / Setup** | "Didn't know I needed AI key first" |
| **Apply flow** | "Preview took too long / unclear when to click send" |
| **Email quality** | "Sounds generic / wrong tone" |
| **Trust & control** | "Scared it would auto-send wrong email" |
| **Missing contact** | "Didn't know how to add recruiter email" |
| **Applications / tracking** | "Couldn't find my app / status confusing" |
| **Extension / LinkedIn** | "Button never showed / connect confusing" |
| **Missing feature** | "No follow-up reminders / no LinkedIn DM" |

**Prioritization rule:** Weight **Active hunters** 2×. A feature mentioned by 1 feedback-only user goes to "maybe"; same feature + friction from 3 active hunters → **next sprint**.

## Facilitation do's & don'ts

**Do**

- Let silence happen — struggle is data
- Ask "show me" instead of "was it confusing?"
- Note **exact UI labels** they mention ("Select status", "Apply", "Needs review")
- Run the **same 7 questions** even if the conversation goes elsewhere

**Don't**

- Defend the product ("yeah we're fixing that") — say "that's helpful, tell me more"
- Lead: ❌ "Was the Applications tab confusing?" → ✅ "What happened when you opened Applications?"
- Demo the whole product — one hint max, then return to their attempt
- Treat "this is cool" as validation — always ask "would you use it Monday on a real job?"

---

## OneTap-specific things to watch

1. **Two modes** — Auto-apply ("draft and send automatically") vs review ("preview and send"). Many users won't understand the toggle; capture which mode they used and whether they noticed it.
2. **Setup gate** — Can't apply without resume + email + AI. Note if they hit this before value.
3. **No email in JD** — App may land in **Needs review**; see if they understand what to do next (add contact, Continue send).
4. **Apply vs Applications** — Two tabs; see if they expect the app to appear instantly and whether they know where to look.
5. **Extension** — Separate setup (Connect in Settings → Chrome Extension). Don't force extension users through paste-JD task only.
